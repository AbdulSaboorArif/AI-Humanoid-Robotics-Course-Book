## Glossary of Terms

- **APA**: American Psychological Association citation style, 7th edition.
- **CoM**: Center of Mass - the point where the total mass of a system is considered to be concentrated.
- **Isaac**: NVIDIA's robotics platform for AI-powered robots.
- **LIDAR**: Light Detection and Ranging - a remote sensing method using light in the form of a pulsed laser.
- **ROS**: Robot Operating System - flexible framework for writing robot software.
- **VLA**: Vision-Language-Action - systems that integrate visual perception, language understanding, and physical action.
- **ZMP**: Zero Moment Point - a point where the moment of the ground reaction force is zero.
